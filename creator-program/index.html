THE CONTENT

Made to feel like you.

Creators must publish content on their own social channels. We agree on Reels, posts, Stories, treatment or results content, posting deadlines, and tags before treatment. Providing content only for Alluring’s channels does not meet the posting requirement.

THE COMMITMENT

12 months. Alluring only.

If you collaborate with us, you may not promote, endorse, or take part in promotional collaborations with any other med spa for 12 months from the collaboration start date stated in your signed agreement. This applies to all med spa services and paid, gifted, or unpaid promotions, not only the treatment you receive at Alluring.

THE TRANSPARENCY

Honest, always.

Share your real experience and clearly disclose gifted or paid collaborations. We don’t ask for scripted praise or guaranteed results.

Show up & follow through.

Attend agreed appointments, communicate scheduling changes promptly, and deliver content and follow-up results updates by the dates in your agreement.

Share the numbers.

Provide genuine audience insights and recent post analytics when applying, then share performance screenshots for your collaboration posts after publication at the agreed reporting dates.

Agree on content use.

Confirm what Alluring can repost or use in marketing before treatment. Any paid advertising rights, content review, and before-and-after permissions are addressed in your agreement.

Before treatment, selected creators sign a separate agreement confirming the 12-month exclusivity dates, content deliverables, posting deadlines, tags, and permissions for Alluring to use the content. Applying acknowledges these expectations; it is not the final partnership agreement.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW
Is there a minimum follower count?+

We look at your audience, engagement, content, and brand alignment together. The application welcomes creators at different stages.

Does applying include complimentary treatment?+

No. Submitting an application does not guarantee selection, a partnership, or complimentary services. Any offer is discussed individually.

Can I work with other med spas?+

Selected creators cannot promote or endorse any other med spa for 12 months from the collaboration start date in their signed agreement. This includes paid, gifted, and unpaid promotions across all med spa services. Disclose existing commitments in your application so we can review potential conflicts before approval.

What should I include with my application?+

Submit your media kit and screenshots or a shareable report of recent post analytics. Include the reporting period, views, reach, likes, comments, shares, saves, audience location, and the women/men breakdown shown in your platform insights. Use the fields below to explain anything your platform does not report.

Do I have to post on my own account?+

Yes. Collaborations require publishing on your own social channels. Content supplied only for Alluring to post is not sufficient. Specific formats, quantities, deadlines, tags, and reporting dates are confirmed before treatment.

What happens to my application?+

Your contact information, social profiles, audience insights, and submitted files are stored for Alluring Image’s partnership review. Do not include medical records or sensitive health information. To request deletion, contact us at 561-567-9056.

When will I hear back?+

Our team reviews applications individually and will contact you if there’s a potential fit. There is no guaranteed response time.
